It makes me sad when I see people write personal things using llms. I am not mad, I am just really sad and I am especially disappointed when someone significant does it. Recently I've been reading a couple of Terrance Tao's blog posts from guests. Tristian Buckmaster there wrote a statement about the future of math, he mentioned at the end AI was used to help write his statement. Honestly, I wasn't surprised, because it felt like it, and many people in the comments felt like it. Someone said that it feels everything I read nowadays feels similiar. I agree, there is nothing to disagree on(though the comments section, at least on youtube and in these blogs, gives a breath of fresh air. The casualness of a comment is more enjoyable and authentic, than a constructive statement, because the pressure to "sound good" is now spoiled by the convenience of a bot). Donald Knuth in a numberphile podcast, that I listened back when I was a freshman, said he tried really hard when he was writing the art of computer programming to produce good writing. He said that while answering a question about what he thought about llms. I don't remember what his answer was, but I probably don't because it was probably generic. But I respect his writing efforts, it's authentic, and that's what should matter. But this was hilarious to read.
